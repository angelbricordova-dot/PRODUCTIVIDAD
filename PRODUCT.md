# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

One person: Alejandro Gabriel Lamont Bencomo. He uses Alba on his iPhone as an app installed on the home screen, several times a day (mornings, afternoons and nights), to keep his habits, discipline and relationships going and to see how he is improving.

## Product Purpose

A personal productivity and life app that turns building good habits into a game. It brings together habits grouped into rituals, a journal in the style of a bullet journal, mood tracking, relationships, goals, and breathing and focus sessions. Success means using it every day without feeling guilty, and seeing his progress in real time (XP, level, streaks, areas of life).

## Positioning

It is made by and for its single user, and it is completely customisable. The game rewards without punishing: XP never goes down, the "never miss twice" rule protects streaks, and coins are exchanged for real rewards the user defines.

## Operating Context

- An iPhone, used as a PWA from the home screen and often with one hand.
- Morning, afternoon and night rituals; quick check-ins throughout the day.
- No accounts and no server: data lives in the device's IndexedDB, and there is a manual backup by exporting a file.
- Deployed on Vercel from the GitHub repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Habits (daily, specific days, N times per week, counters, difficulty, trigger, reason), rituals with a guided mode, streaks with a grace day, rest days.
- Journal: intention, mood, gratitude, bullet log with migration, reflection, weekly review.
- People: contact frequency, birthdays, notes, history of interactions.
- Goals with milestones; breathing and focus sessions.
- Game: XP, levels and titles, coins, rewards shop, daily quests, achievements, life-area radar.
- Photos and videos in the journal, profile photo, photos of people, and photo proof of a habit. Every image is compressed on the device before it is saved.
- **Finances are out of scope.** The user already has another app for them. Do not add expenses, budgets or savings.
- Typography and colours can be chosen by the user.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is **Alba** and it is final.
- Language: Spanish, written to the user as "tú", warm, close and encouraging, without guilt.
- References the user named: Headspace and Fabulous; playful, illustrated, like a video game (direction "Ilustrado juguetón").

## Evidence on Hand

There are no testimonials, metrics or external claims. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Reward, never punish.
2. Every action takes seconds and works with one thumb.
3. Your data is yours: local, exportable, light.
4. Completely customisable: name, avatar, areas, habits, rewards, typography, colour.
5. It should feel nice to come back to every day.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text that is readable at the iPhone's size, with an adjustable text size, sufficient contrast, and respect for "reduce motion".
